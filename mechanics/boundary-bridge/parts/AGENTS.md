## Boundary Bridge Parts Route

Use these parts when the SDK exposes a typed bridge over source-owned sibling
surfaces without taking their meaning.

Active part homes:

- `consumed-surface-posture-gate/` checks whether sibling-owned surfaces are
  safe to read before SDK facades trust them.
- `skill-environment-inspector/` keeps exact owner surfaces and user,
  repository, legacy-workspace, and source-export observations separate without
  selecting or executing capabilities.
- `technique-promotion-readiness-reader/` keeps promotion-readiness reads below
  `aoa-techniques` meaning and review authority.
- `owner-layer-signal-handoff/` keeps advisory surface detection and reviewed
  owner-layer handoff material below activation or dispatch authority.
- `organ-access-control-plane/` projects one explicit private registry into
  discovery and candidate-only activation plans.
- `route-resolution-control-plane/` resolves and explains C1 route candidates
  from receipt-bound routing and exact owner projections without plan
  compilation, activation, or execution.

Keep source-owned truth in sibling repositories. Outside the separately
contracted C1 resolver, SDK parts may expose handles, labels, and reviewed
handoff packets only. The C1 part may additionally expose deterministic
non-executing route decisions. Runtime adapter selection and execution belong
to the host; capability meaning stays with its owner, and task-local DAG
composition stays with the executing agent until a separately contracted
compiler owns a bounded plan contour.
